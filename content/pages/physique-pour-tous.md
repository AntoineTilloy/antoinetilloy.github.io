"*Physique pour tous*" was a physics course given at *École Normale Supérieure* in Paris to students in humanities. I was responsible for the course from 2013 to 2016 but I was greatly helped (among other people) by Antoine Bourget, Irénée Frérot and Pierre Ronceray. The unfinished lecture notes *en français* for the year 2015 can be found bellow.
