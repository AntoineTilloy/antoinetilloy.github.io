AI is deeply changing the way we do theoretical research. We are entering a period of massive acceleration, which comes with both a lot of enthusiasm and a lot of anxiety. These changes are coming fast, with a characteristic timescale of a few months. Predicting what our jobs may look like in the next year is thus impossible.

In this context, I can no longer recruit PhD students and postdocs to work on a precise project. This would be dishonest. I still think there is a need to train the next generation of humans. I may thus consider exceptionally creative and ambitious people, who are willing to "ride the AI dragon" until it is no longer possible. The main drive should be to push for discoveries, and understanding, not to train for what is already gone.

If your views align, you may contact me for inquiries. Tell me in a few lines what you want to discover, and tell me about your use of AI. I receive a lot of emails asking for positions, so I will likely not answer if I think the fit is most likely not right.
