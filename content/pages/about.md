I am currently (the equivalent of) associate professor in the *Centre Automatique et Systèmes* at *Mines Paris*, which is part of Paris Sciences et Lettres (PSL), and lead the [Quantic](https://www.inria.fr/en/quantic) research team physically located at Inria. [AI is profoundly changing the way I work](/working-with-me-2/), but I remain interested in all aspects of quantum mechanics: orthodox (figuring out how to compute stuff) and unorthodox (understanding what the stuff we compute means about the world). These days, I am mostly interested in tensor networks (which I use to compress complex many-body quantum states) and quantum field theory (which I try to solve non-perturbatively with tensor networks). I also collaborate with the company [*Alice & Bob*](https://alice-bob.com/) on a wide range of questions, mostly related to the continuous measurement and control of quantum systems. Finally, I keep side interests in semiclassical/quantum gravity, quantum foundations, and nuclear engineering.

I defended my *Habilitation à diriger les recherches* on relativistic continuous matrix product states (RCMPS), which is a new variational method for low-dimensional quantum field theory ([arXiv:2604.10284](https://arxiv.org/abs/2604.10284)).

Before coming back to Paris, I was a postdoctoral researcher at the *Max Planck Institute of Quantum Optics* near Munich, in the theory division led by Ignacio Cirac. This is where I started getting interested in tensor networks, and their use in quantum field theory.

![Writing on a blackboard covered in equations and diagrams](/wp-content/uploads/2016/09/cover_resized.jpg)

Even before that, I did my PhD at *École Normale Supérieure* in Paris under the supervision of Denis Bernard. It was devoted mainly to mathematical physics problems related to continuous quantum measurement with a few excursions in foundations and quantum information theory. The PhD thesis (in French) can be downloaded on [HAL](https://hal.science/tel-01341922).
