The preprint on subcritical reactors I had written about two months ago has finally appeared on [arxiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/2012.01566). Apparently it was "on hold" for that long because the primary category (applied physics) was not the right one, and the moderators thought "instrumentation and detectors" was better. Honestly, I am not sure they are right, given how theoretical the paper is, but I am just glad the paper appeared (I would have just preferred it to be faster).
