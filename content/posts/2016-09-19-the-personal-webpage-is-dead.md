I have decided to switch from a standard personal webpage, that has to be migrated everytime I change of institution, to a sort of research log -hosted outside- that will follow me along the years. This is an opportunity to do some outreach and show the subjects I am working on but also to give a researcher's point of view about a few subjects on the news. There are quite a few good physics blogs out there (*e.g.* [Woit](http://www.math.columbia.edu/~woit/wordpress/)'s, [Aaronson](http://www.scottaaronson.com/blog/)'s, [Hossenfelder](http://backreaction.blogspot.fr/)'s), my objective is only to have a slightly improved personal webpage to begin with.
