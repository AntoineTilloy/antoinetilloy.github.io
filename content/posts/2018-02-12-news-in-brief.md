I have finally decided to put on [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1802.03291) a slightly remastered version (with figures) of my submission to the Beyond Spacetime [essay contest](https://beyondspacetime.net/2017-essay-prize-contest/). I have slightly tamed the provocative tone but it remains a bit rough. I still post it because I think it puts together many arguments I often make informally in seminars.

The winning essays took a slightly less head on approach to the subject of the contest, which was "*Space and Time After Quantum Gravity*":

- *Why Black Hole Information Loss is Paradoxical*, by David Wallace, [arXiv](https://arxiv.org/abs/1710.03783)
- *Problems with the cosmological constant problem*, by Adam Koberinski, [PhilSci](http://philsci-archive.pitt.edu/14244/)

Wallace's article is an answer to Tim Maudlin's article I had mentioned [here](/2017/06/08/potpourri-of-articles-ii/), but I have not read it yet.
