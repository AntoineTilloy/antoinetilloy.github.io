A bit more than a month ago, I put on arxiv two preprints ([short](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.07733) and [long](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.07741)) that summarize my recent work on applying the variational method in relativistic quantum field theory. I am happy that they were (so far) well received, and I got the chance to present the corresponding results in a few seminars already. The last two, at the University of Helsinki and EPFL, were recorded. I had a better microphone at EPFL, and expanded more on tensor networks, so I embed the video below ([or direct link here](https://tube.switch.ch/videos/0hSQb2G7MU)).
